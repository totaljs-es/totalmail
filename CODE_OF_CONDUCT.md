# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this, and may block people who keep doing it.

To report a problem, use the private reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact the maintainers through GitHub.
